Privacy Policy

MMC Live Signal provides market-data analysis and MMC-based trading signals.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Information we use

The service uses the login credentials supplied by the account owner to protect access. When a user requests a signal, the service processes the selected market and timeframes needed to calculate the signal.

Market data

For supported real-market and crypto modes, the service requests market candle data from its configured market-data providers. The selected market is fetched on demand when the user requests a signal; the app is not designed to continuously analyze every market.

Device permissions

The Android app requires internet access to load the MMC Live Signal web service. It does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, SMS, or phone calls.

Cookies and sessions

The web service uses a secure login session cookie to keep an authenticated user signed in. The session cookie is not used for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared with external services only as necessary to provide the requested market-data functionality and host the service.

Security

Access is protected by account authentication and HTTPS. Users should keep their login credentials private.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-related requests, contact the app owner through the support contact provided with the app listing.
