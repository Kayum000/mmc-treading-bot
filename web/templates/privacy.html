Privacy Policy

MMC Live Signal provides market-data analysis and MMC-based trading signals.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Information we use

The service uses the login credentials supplied by the account owner to protect access. When a user requests a signal, the service processes the selected market and timeframe needed to calculate the signal.

Market data

Real Forex signals use configured market data. Quotex OTC signals use normalized candle data collected from the user's visible Quotex chart. The service does not receive or store a Quotex password, session identifier, or order action.

Device permissions

The Android collector requires screen capture permission and USB debugging/ADB access when used from a PC. It reads the visible chart only and does not place trades.

Cookies and sessions

The web service uses a secure login session cookie to keep an authenticated user signed in.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only as necessary to provide requested market-data functionality and host the service.

Security

Access is protected by authentication and HTTPS. Keep login credentials and the ingestion secret private.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-related requests, contact the app owner through the support contact provided with the app listing.
